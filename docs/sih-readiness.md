# SihFlow ERP — SIH Grand Finale Readiness Evaluation Guide

## 1. Objective
This document outlines the **14 Evaluation Categories** used by the SihFlow ERP to assess the hackathon readiness of **AcadShield** for the Smart India Hackathon Grand Finale.

---
